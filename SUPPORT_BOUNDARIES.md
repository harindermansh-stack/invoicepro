# InvoicePro Support Boundaries

## Included

Reasonable setup guidance may be requested through the creator-contact option associated with your Gumroad purchase. Included guidance covers:

- Locating the included documentation
- Clarifying the documented installation steps
- Helping identify the relevant part of a Vercel build error
- Clarifying which environment variable relates to an optional integration

Support is provided on a reasonable-effort basis. Response time is not guaranteed.

## Not included

- Managed installation or deployment performed for you
- Custom development, branding or feature changes
- Migration of customer or accounting data
- Configuration or troubleshooting of third-party accounts
- Security audits, penetration testing or compliance certification
- Bookkeeping, tax, legal or invoice-validity advice
- Guaranteed compatibility with every hosting provider, browser or future dependency version
- Guaranteed uptime, future updates or lifetime maintenance

Never send passwords, API keys, authentication tokens, private customer data or full `.env.local` files when requesting help.

## Refunds

The refund terms displayed by Gumroad at the time of purchase govern the transaction. Any summary on a marketing page should not expand or contradict those checkout terms.
