# InvoicePro Verified Features and Requirements

## Verified core capabilities

- Live invoice preview while editing
- PDF generation and download
- Default and Stripe-inspired invoice designs
- Seller and buyer profile reuse
- VAT, GST, sales-tax and custom tax-label support
- Automatic invoice calculations
- Logo support
- Custom QR-code content, including payment links or other invoice-related information
- Multi-page PDF pagination
- 10+ interface languages
- 120+ currencies
- Mobile-friendly interface
- No sign-up required for the core browser workflow
- Browser/local-storage-oriented handling for core invoice data

## Important wording limits

- The package does not claim five premium templates; two invoice designs are verified in the packaged version.
- QR codes are customizable, but direct Stripe or PayPal payment processing is not included or verified as a built-in integration.
- Seller and buyer profiles are not described as a full customer relationship management system.
- No five-minute setup time is promised.
- No lifetime-update promise is included.
- No private community access is included.

## Technology
